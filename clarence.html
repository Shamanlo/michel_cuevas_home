Clarence FAQs

What does the average water damage restoration cost in Iowa?

Based on 2026 industry data, most Iowa homeowners pay roughly $1,315 to $6,065 for water damage restoration, with the average job landing around $3,675. Mitigation—the extraction and drying phase—typically runs about $2.85 to $7.15 per square foot, and full repair and rebuild work can add roughly $19 to $35 per square foot on top of that. The water category and how fast drying starts drive the final number, which is why calling us within the first 24 to 48 hours almost always saves money. We put every scope in writing and work directly with your insurance.
