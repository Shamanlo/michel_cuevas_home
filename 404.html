About Us

Iowa City's Roofer, The Right Way

Cuevas Home Improvement has been working out of Iowa City since 1997. Decades in one corridor means we've seen what this climate does to a building. Hail cracks shingles, ice dams back water up under the eaves, and the freeze-thaw cycle lifts concrete and pulls gutters loose.

Everything we install is picked for that. We're a family business that still runs into its customers around town, and that keeps us honest.

  • Straight quotes. The number on the estimate is the number on the invoice.
  • Our own crews. No subcontractors on your roof.
  • We clean up. Magnet sweep and haul-off on every job.

Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer 0% interest financing on qualifying projects, with no payments due for the first 30 days. We go over your options during the free estimate, and there's no pressure to use it if you'd rather pay another way. Every quote comes in writing either way.

How much does a free estimate cost?

Nothing, it's completely free with no obligation to hire us afterward. We come out, walk the whole project with you, and put the price and full scope in writing before you decide anything. That written number is the number: the price doesn't change once we start unless you ask us to change the scope, or we open up a wall or a roof and find hidden damage underneath—and if that happens we stop and talk it through with you before any extra work or cost. You're never surprised by the invoice.

How much does a new roof cost in Iowa City, IA?

A full residential roof replacement in the Iowa City area typically runs between about $7,840 and $19,600, with most standard-sized homes landing in the $9,800 to $14,700 range. The final number depends on the size and pitch of your roof, the material you choose, and how much decking or hidden damage we find once the old roof comes off. We give you an exact written price during the free estimate—not a guess—and we'll always tell you honestly whether you need a full replacement or just a repair.

Are you licensed and insured?

We're licensed, insured, and bonded. You get proof of insurance before any crew sets a ladder against your house. Full liability coverage is in place on every job and every crew member.

What areas do you cover?

We work out of Iowa City and cover the whole Corridor: Iowa City, Coralville, North Liberty, Cedar Rapids, West Liberty, and Washington. If you're just outside one of those towns, call us anyway.

How soon can you start my project?

It depends on the scope and the season. Smaller repairs and emergency calls we often get to within a day or two. Full reroofs, siding jobs, and remodels usually get scheduled a bit further out during peak spring and summer months. We give you a real start date during the estimate, not a vague window.
