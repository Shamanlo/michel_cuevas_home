Information We Collect

When you request an estimate, contact us, or fill out a form on this website, we collect the information you provide directly, which may include your name, phone number, email address, mailing address, and details about your project.

Our website also collects limited technical information automatically, such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited, through standard web server logs. We do not use this information to personally identify you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your estimate request, schedule and perform work, send project updates, and answer questions. If you consent to receive calls or text messages, we may also contact you about your project by phone or SMS. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or contact us directly at (319) 333-5046.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information with other companies for their own marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses a small number of third-party services to operate. Form submissions are processed through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service that sends your submission to our email inbox. Our homepage video is hosted through YouTube, and our footer map is provided through Google Maps. These providers may collect standard technical data (such as IP address) when their embedded content loads, under their own privacy policies.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell data to ad networks.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies to track you across other websites. Embedded third-party content, such as the YouTube video player and Google Maps embed on this site, may set their own cookies when loaded, governed by YouTube's and Google's respective privacy policies.

Data Retention & Security

We retain the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to complete your project, respond to your inquiry, and maintain business records, including for warranty and insurance purposes. We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, though no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to update or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at micuevas1986@gmail.com or (319) 333-5046. If you no longer wish to receive calls, texts, or emails from us about a project, let us know and we will honor that request.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking home improvement services and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
